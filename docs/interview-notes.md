
# Project B – Regulatory Change Impact Interview

**Interview Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Interviewee:** Compliance and Operations Manager

## Main Problem
The manager spends too much time reviewing inconsistent AI system records and preparing compliance review packages.

## Current Process
- Review AI system information and applicable legal requirements.
- Check evidence and identify missing information.
- Record compliance findings in the impact register.
- Assign responsibilities to system owners, Legal, and Operations.
- Track deadlines in the action calendar.
- Prepare a review package for Legal and Operations.

## Problems Identified
- Information is scattered across multiple registers.
- Some evidence is missing or incomplete.
- Different documents may contain conflicting information.
- Tracking changes and deadlines takes time.

## Requested AI Tool Features
- Detect missing evidence and conflicting information.
- Maintain consistency across documents.
- Track changes in the impact register and action calendar.
- Identify unresolved compliance issues.
- Prepare review summaries for Legal and Operations.
- Preserve previous versions and change history.

## Important Rules
- Legal makes final legal interpretations.
- Operations controls activation and approved deadlines.
- Unresolved legal questions must not be bypassed.
- The tool must not automatically mark incomplete evidence as compliant.
- All changes and corrections must remain traceable.
